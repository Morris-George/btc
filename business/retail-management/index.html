Retail Management

BTC operates its own retail network of stores, boutiques and service centers across Egypt.

BTC Owned Retail Network

BTC’s materials describe a BTC owned retail network that includes BTC Exclusive and BTC stores, brand boutiques, Link stores, Casio outlets, BTC DFS airport outlets, a service center, Pandora stores and a Läderach boutique.

BTC airport outlets are available at all departure terminals of Cairo, Hurghada and Sharm El Sheikh airports.
