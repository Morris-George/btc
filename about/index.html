How It Started

It all started in 1935, when Mr. Loria Wassef, founder of the renowned market leader known today as BTC Group, opened his first boutique under the name “Loria Shop” in Fayoum.

“It’s my love to watches that drove me to become a watchmaker. What intrigues me about watch making is my fascination with its punctuality, which made me want to discover the watch world further and know more about it.”

Mr. Loria Wassef, Founder

BTC group is an entirely family owned group of companies, whose seed was founded in 1935 as a watch and jewelry boutique under the name “Lauria shop”, named after Mr. Lauria Wassef the senior member of the family at Fayoum city.

This seed flourished and grew into BTC group of companies, one of Egypt’s pioneer and leading companies in watches, jewelry and electronics (distributors and manufacturers of Panasonic in Egypt).

By 1976, BTC Trading had become Egypt’s sole distributor for several multinational watch companies, including Swiss brands (from the renowned Swatch group, Movado Group and others). The company is now the market leader of the watch industry in Egypt, offering more than thirty watch and jewelry brands ranging from high-quality, affordable brands through to the most expensive and luxurious.

With over 40 retail outlets and 3 service centers, BTC Trading is Egypt’s largest watch distributor, whose primary concern is to gain and maintain its customers’ trust. To this end, the company’s cooperative team works together to ensure their loyal clients receive the highest quality customer service and satisfaction.

BTC spares no effort or expense on continual modernization and expansion in order to maintain the rapid and stable growth of the market, keep pace with the perpetual marathon of technological development, and reach as many clients as possible throughout Egypt.

Company Background

By 1976, BTC Trading had become Egypt’s sole distributor for several multinational watch companies, including Swiss brands. BTC has been the major distributor for Swatch Group in Egypt since 1974.

The company is now the market leader of the watch industry in Egypt, offering an extended line of watches and jewelry brands ranging from high-quality, affordable brands through to the most expensive and luxurious. Today, BTC is proudly Egypt’s largest watch distributor.
