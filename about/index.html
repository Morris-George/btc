About Us

A Success Story That Started 90 Years Ago

For over 90 years, BTC Group — the family-owned group of companies — has been leading the watch and jewelry market in Egypt, offering a broad range of brands, from watches and jewelry to affordable, trendy brands.

How It Started

It all started in 1935, when Mr. Loria Wassef, founder of the renowned market leader known today as BTC Group, opened his first boutique under the name “Loria Shop” in Fayoum.

Over more than 90 years it has expanded to become one of Egypt’s sole watch retailing companies, with thirty-five retail outlets and service centers all over Egypt.

“It’s my love to watches that drove me to become a watchmaker. What intrigues me about watch making is my fascination with its punctuality, which made me want to discover the watch world further and know more about it.”

Mr. Loria Wassef, Founder

Company Background

By 1976, BTC Trading had become Egypt’s sole distributor for several multinational watch companies, including Swiss brands. BTC has been the major distributor for Swatch Group in Egypt since 1974.

The company is now the market leader of the watch industry in Egypt, offering an extended line of watches and jewelry brands ranging from high-quality, affordable brands through to the most expensive and luxurious. Today, BTC is proudly Egypt’s largest watch distributor.

Business Model, Expertise and Presence

Business Model

BTC distributes international watch and jewelry brands in Egypt, and operates its own retail network and retail concepts, including Link Watches.

Areas of Expertise

Watches and jewelry, from high-quality, affordable brands to the most luxurious. The group also includes BTC Electronics and All Niche, under which Pandora and Läderach operate.

Market Presence

Retail outlets and service centers across Egypt, including Cairo, Giza and Alexandria, and BTC DFS outlets at Cairo Airport terminals.

Company Philosophy

A fundamental factor in the success of the company is its dedicated staff and loyal clients, who are considered not merely customers, but “partners in success.” BTC’s primary concern is gaining and maintaining client trust.

BTC spares no effort or expense on continual modernization and expansion, to maintain the rapid and steady growth of the company, keep pace with technological development, and reach as many clients as possible throughout Egypt.

What Differentiates BTC

Diversity of Brands

The diversity of the brands and their different price ranges suits everyone, which makes BTC the optimum option for any watch seeker, in the words of the founder.

Sole and Major Distribution

BTC is the sole distributor in Egypt of brands including Corum, Gucci watches and jewelry, Raymond Weil and Bulgari.

Customer Satisfaction

A dedicated department for customer satisfaction, and a cooperative team working to ensure clients receive the highest quality of service.

Corporate Positioning

Market leader of the watch industry in Egypt, and Egypt’s largest watch distributor.
