Our Business Philosophy

BTC considers its dedicated staff and loyal clients not merely customers, but “partners in success.” The company’s primary concern is gaining and maintaining client trust.

Our Commitment

BTC’s cooperative team works together to ensure loyal clients receive the highest quality customer service and satisfaction. A dedicated department sends all customers a quarterly mail informing them about new products and special offers.

Our Long-Term Direction

From the CEO’s letter: BTC continues to evolve by expanding its presence across Egypt, deepening its partnerships and reimagining the luxury retail experience for a new generation, through new boutiques, enhanced digital platforms and collaborations with leading global brands. Its vision is to make world-class watchmaking more accessible while maintaining the personalized, premium service that defines the brand.
