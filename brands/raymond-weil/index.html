Raymond Weil

Brand Introduction

Raymond Weil is a Geneva-based, family-owned watchmaker. Its millesime small seconds won the 2023 Grand Prix d’Horlogerie de Genève (GPHG) in the Challenge category, and the brand nears its 50th anniversary in 2026.
