Läderach

Brand Introduction

Läderach is a famous Swiss luxury chocolate maker founded in 1962 by Rudolf Läderach Jr. in Glarus, Switzerland.

It is known for FrischSchoggi (fresh chocolate) sold in large slabs, along with pralines, truffles and artisan snack items. Läderach controls the entire production process from sourcing raw cocoa beans to final retail sales in its own branded shops.

It operates over 250 chocolateries worldwide across more than 30 countries, including a location in Cairo, Egypt, and is run by the third generation of the Läderach family (Johannes, Elias and David Läderach).
