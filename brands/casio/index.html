Casio

Brand Introduction

Founded in 1946, Casio describes its aim as bringing new levels of joy to lives, one by one, by putting the power of wonder at hand.

Casio Creativity is inspired and driven by the individual to find and satisfy unfelt needs, combining original thinking, cutting-edge technology and passion to create new and wonder-filled experiences. Casio Contribution is the nurturing of new value that enlivens and enriches global society, with products and services that spark curiosity and creativity.
